How It Works

Simple structure. Honest check-ins. No fluff.

1

Map It

We start with a free discovery call. You bring the goal; we break it into clear, weekly actions you can actually do.

2

Meet Weekly

A focused check-in every week. What got done, what didn't, and why — no judgment, just honest review.

3

Follow Through

Between sessions you have direct access for course-correction. Excuses get named. Progress gets celebrated. Momentum builds.

Coaching is not therapy.

Coaching is forward-looking and action-oriented: we work on your goals, habits, and follow-through. It doesn't diagnose or treat mental health conditions. If you need therapeutic support, I'll be the first to say so — and I can point you toward the right resources.

Questions

The ones everyone asks.

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching is forward-looking and action-focused — we work on goals, habits, and follow-through. Therapy addresses mental health diagnosis and treatment. If what you need is therapeutic, I'll tell you honestly and help you find the right support.

How much time does this take each week?

One focused session per week (typically 30–45 minutes), plus the actions we agree on. The whole point is fitting progress into a busy life — not adding another burden to it.

What if I fall off track?

Then we talk about it honestly — that's literally what I'm here for. No shame, no lectures. We figure out what broke down, fix the system, and keep walking.

Are you a certified coach?

I'm currently completing my ICF-aligned Level 1 coach training through Tandem Coaching Academy. My founding clients get experienced, structured coaching at founding rates while I complete certification.

How do sessions happen?

By phone or video call — whatever fits your schedule, including evenings. Direct messaging between sessions keeps you on track day to day.
