My Story
Read the transcript

I remember a call, years ago, middle of the night, rain coming down sideways. We pulled up to a house where everything had gone wrong at once, and in the middle of it there was this calm — one person holding everything together for everybody else. I never forgot that. Somebody has to be the steady one. That's what fifteen years in emergency work teaches you. Not just medicine. Composure. How to walk into chaos and slow it down. How to make the next right decision when nobody has the full picture. I carried those lessons out of the field and into everything I do now. The situations are different — a degree, a business, a habit, a life being rebuilt — but the work is the same. Show up. Tell the truth. Take the next step.
